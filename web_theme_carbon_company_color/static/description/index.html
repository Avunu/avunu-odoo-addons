Carbon Backend Theme / Company Color

Per-company brand colour, expressed in Carbon tokens

Glue between Carbon Backend Theme and OCA's Web Company Color. Installs itself automatically when both are present.

Without it the two fight: web_company_color emits its own palette as !important CSS loaded after everything else, and simply overwrites the Carbon theme. With it, the colours a company sets are re-expressed as Carbon design tokens, so the whole theme — buttons, links, focus rings, selected states, tabs, the side nav rule — follows from them coherently.
